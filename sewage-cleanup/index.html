Few emergencies in a building are as hazardous as sewage backing up into it. Anyone who comes in contact with contaminated material is exposed to parasites, bacteria and viruses, among them giardia, E. coli, salmonella and hepatitis, all carried in raw sewage and all dangerous. San Mateo Restoration provides emergency sewage cleanup throughout San Mateo, responding 24/7 with properly trained technicians, appropriate personal protective equipment and EPA-registered disinfectants to decontaminate your property safely.

Water damage comes in categories, and sewage sits in the worst one: Category 3, called black water, the highest level of contamination and danger. That rating calls for the strictest protocols: containment barriers go up, crews wear full protective equipment, every porous material the sewage touched is removed, and the surfaces that stay are disinfected thoroughly. For Category 3 water damage the reference is IICRC S500, and our crews follow it to bring the building back to safe occupancy.

We clear the standing sewage first, then pull contaminated water out of structural materials, remove carpet, padding, insulation, drywall and any other porous item that was in contact with sewage for proper disposal, disinfect each hard surface, treat with antimicrobial products and finish with structural drying, which prevents secondary mold. To finish, thermal foggers and hydroxyl generators of industrial grade deodorize the space so the sewage smell does not linger.

What a Professional Brings to Sewage Cleanup

Sewage cleanup is not something any homeowner should attempt. The biological hazards in raw sewage require specialized training, proper personal protective equipment (Tyvek suits, respirators, protective gloves and boots) and EPA-registered disinfection products that are not available to consumers. Hepatitis, gastrointestinal disease and infections of the skin or respiratory system are among the serious illnesses these pathogens cause.

Disposal matters as well, since everything the sewage contaminated has to be handled as biohazard waste. Simply placing sewage-contaminated drywall, carpet and insulation in your regular trash is both illegal and hazardous to sanitation workers and the community. A certified restoration company follows proper disposal protocols and works with licensed facilities that accept biohazard waste.

How to Prevent Sewage Cleanup Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Keep cooking oil, grease and food scraps out of the drain, where they build into clogs
  • Toilet paper is the only thing to flush, and that rules out wipes sold as flushable, which still clog
  • In an emergency a plumber needs the cleanout, so learn where yours is and keep it in good shape
  • Stormwater from a sump or downspout should never drain into the sewer, since the extra volume invites a backup
  • A drain that is slowing down should be dealt with early, while it is still partly open
  • Have roots removed where trees grow by the sewer, before they get inside the pipe and plug it

Warning Signs for Sewage Cleanup

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Slow drains throughout the house that plunging does not fix
  • Dark, dirty water or sewage rising out of a tub, toilet or floor drain
  • Soggy, smelly ground over the sewer line or leakage at the cleanout cap
  • Carpet or flooring contaminated when a toilet overflowed with solid waste
  • Several fixtures backing up at once instead of only one
  • Wet spots on floors or walls accompanied by foul odors
  • Lower levels or drain openings giving off a heavy sewage odor, sometimes like rotten eggs
